Before you ask

Questions I get on every first call

How long until I actually see results?

You'll see the audit and early fixes in weeks 1–2. Real ranking movement typically starts around month 2–3, and compounds from there. Anything faster is usually paid ads, not SEO.

What do you need from me each month?

Mostly just access (site login, analytics) up front, and about 30 minutes a month for our check-in call. I handle the execution — you stay looped in, not buried in tasks.

Can I switch packages later?

Yes — most clients start at Starter or Growth and move up once they see the early wins. We revisit this together every quarter.

What if it doesn't work?

I can't promise rankings — that's Google's call, not mine. What I do guarantee is a visibly, provably better website by month 6: technical health, content, and authority, all documented in your monthly reports. If that improvement isn't there, I'll make it right. See the guarantee above for the full picture.

Will this get me a specific number of customers?

Honestly — I can't guarantee that, and no one credibly can. My job is to make your website far more visible and competitive than it is today. What people do once they find you is where marketing meets your product, pricing, and service.
